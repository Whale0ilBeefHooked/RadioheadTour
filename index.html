RAC Arena (Perth) In-The-Round Seating Guide

Evaluating seating options for the upcoming Perth shows at RAC Arena using the official arena configuration:

🏟️ RAC Arena (Perth Arena) Configuration Analysis

🗺️ View Official RAC Arena Seating Map (Austadiums)

Floor GA (General Admission Standing)

  • Intimacy Advantage: At RAC Arena, the floor dimensions mean you are never more than 15–20 meters from the central stage rail anywhere on the standing floor.
  • 360° Rail Perimeter: The circular stage expands the front-row rail by nearly 3× compared to a traditional end-stage, giving vastly more fans rail-side proximity.
  • Acoustic Punch: The floor receives direct output from the suspended overhead circular line-array speaker cluster with zero slapback delay.

Lower Bowl (Sections 001–012 / Ground & Level 1)

  • The "Sweet Spot" (Rows E to N): Provides an elevated 30-degree perspective looking directly into the cylindrical mesh screens and down onto the circular stage floor.
  • Zero "Restricted View" Seats: Traditional end-stage shows curtain off sections 001, 002, 009, and 010 behind the stage. In-the-round opens all sections 360° with completely unobstructed sightlines.
  • Full Visual Production: The best vantage point to appreciate the 3D moving screen choreography and light sculptures.
💡 Comparison with Sydney & Melbourne: Qudos Bank Arena (Sydney) and Rod Laver Arena (Melbourne) share identical bowl acoustics for in-the-round layouts. If buying seated tickets in any Australian city, prioritize mid-elevation lower bowl seats (Rows 10–20) directly perpendicular to the arena center for the most immersive balance of sound and multimedia art.

Key Evolution Findings

  • Opener Shift: Let Down opened in Madrid, but became an encore centerpiece in London. Template A opened with Planet Telex and Template B opened with 2+2=5.
  • Madrid Exclusives: Madrid received Subterranean Homesick Alien and How to Disappear Completely, which were rotated out before London.

The 7-Year Hiatus (2018–2025) & Setlist Evolution

The 7-Year Hiatus Breakdown (2,287 Days)

  • Last Concert Before Hiatus: 1 August 2018 at Wells Fargo Center in Philadelphia, PA (culmination of the 2-year A Moon Shaped Pool world tour).
  • Why the Long Break? The band experienced severe post-tour burnout. As Ed O'Brien and Philip Selway later stated in The Times, the band felt "disconnected and spent" and needed to step away to preserve their relationships.
  • Side Projects (2021–2024): Thom Yorke and Jonny Greenwood formed The Smile with drummer Tom Skinner, releasing 3 critically acclaimed albums (A Light for Attracting Attention, Wall of Eyes, Cutouts). Ed toured his solo LP (Earth), Philip released solo/orchestral works, and Colin toured on bass with Nick Cave.
  • The "In-The-Round" Origin (Jan 2022): The 360° circular stage was NOT born in Radiohead — it was tested by Thom and Jonny at The Smile's debut at Magazine London (three 360° in-the-round shows in 24 hours). Its electric intimacy directly inspired Radiohead's 2025 arena staging.

2018 (Traditional End-Stage) vs. 2025 (In-the-Round)

Final Pre-Hiatus Show (Philadelphia, 1 Aug 2018):
Opened with 3 straight ambient tracks (Daydreaming → Desert Island Disk → Ful Stop), followed by a loose, un-templated mix of classics, closing on Karma Police. Staging relied on traditional proscenium end-stage lighting grids.

The 2025 Transformation:
✓ Replaced loose improvisation with the high-precision Template A / B Alternating Architecture.
✓ Upgraded from traditional end-stage to a 360° central island with 3D moving mesh screens.
✓ Dropped guitar cables entirely for low-latency digital wireless packs.
✓ Added touring drummer Chris Vatalaro (replacing Clive Deamer) for dual-kit polyrhythms.

Why the 360° Setup Was a Game-Changer

  • Ditching the Guitar Cables: Guitar World reported that after 30+ years of refusing wireless systems, custom ultra-wideband transmitters convinced Jonny, Thom, and Ed to cut their cables, allowing them to roam and face different audience quadrants constantly.
  • Chris Vatalaro: The acclaimed percussionist took over the second drum kit position from Clive Deamer, driving the polymetric rhythms on Bloom, 15 Step, and Ful Stop.
  • Record-Breaking Capacity: By eliminating the dead space behind a standard proscenium stage, The O2 welcomed 22,355 fans on Night 4, the largest concert crowd in the venue's history.
  • Human Rights Coda: Each night closed with the overhead circular video screens scrolling the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

The "A/B" Setlist Blueprint Mechanics

Template A (Nights 1 & 3)

Atmospheric Arc
  • Opener Suite: Planet Telex → 2+2=5 → Sit Down. Stand Up. → Lucky
  • Electronic / Poly Core: Bloom, 15 Step, The Gloaming, Kid A, Videotape, The National Anthem
  • Encore Climax: Fake Plastic Trees opener, Paranoid Android, Just, and always concludes with the communal singalong of Karma Police.

Template B (Nights 2 & 4)

Anthemic Guitar Arc
  • Opener Suite: 2+2=5 → Airbag → Jigsaw Falling Into Place → All I Need
  • The Fixed 18-Song Engine: In London, Night 2 and Night 4 played the exact same 18 main songs in order, culminating in Exit Music (for a Film) into Street Spirit (Fade Out)!
  • Encore Climax: Explodes with Let Down → Weird Fishes → Idioteque → Present Tense, featuring the rare piano slot (The Daily Mail / Like Spinning Plates) and closing on There There.

2027 Australian Residency Strategic Guide (Perth, Sydney, Melbourne)

🎯 The Golden Ticketing Rule for Australian Fans

Radiohead announced multi-night residencies for Perth, Sydney, and Melbourne in May & June 2027. Because they operate on the strict A/B Template System:

  • ALWAYS BUY TWO CONSECUTIVE NIGHTS (N1+N2 or N3+N4): You will experience 37 to 40 UNIQUE SONGS across 50 performances (over 90% of their rehearsed live catalog). You get the ambient/electronic journey of Set A AND the stadium-guitar catharsis of Set B.
  • DO NOT BUY NON-CONSECUTIVE ODD NIGHTS (e.g., N1 + N3): You will get an ~85% duplicate setlist.
  • FLOOR vs SEATED SIGHTLINES: Floor GA offers 360° rail access right next to the band. Lower Bowl (Rows 10–20) offers the optimal panoramic sightline of the descending 3D cylindrical screens. There are zero obstructed view seats anywhere in the arena.
  • ANTI-TOUTING RULES: Tickets will be strictly tied to purchaser ID via digital barcodes. Registration with postcode verification on Radiohead’s official portal will be required.
